I am an MSc Computer Science student specializing in **Autonomous Systems** at the **University of Stuttgart**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Medical AI**. I am particularly interested in developing robust AI systems that can generalize across real-world environments.

## Research Interests

My current interests include:

- **Computer Vision**
- **Medical Image Analysis**
- **Deep Learning**
- **Domain Generalization**
- **Physics-inspired Machine Learning**
- **Autonomous Systems**

I am interested in understanding how machine learning models can become more reliable when deployed outside controlled research environments, especially in applications where robustness and interpretability are essential.

## Current Projects

### Physics-Inspired Computer Vision for Medical AI

I am currently exploring methods for improving the robustness of medical AI systems against domain shift. My work focuses on understanding physics-inspired preprocessing approaches that standardize medical images across different hospitals and imaging conditions.

This includes studying:

- optical physics-based image transformations,
- Fourier-domain representations,
- phase-based image processing,
- and their impact on deep learning generalization.

### Vision Transformer Continual Learning

I am also working on continual learning methods for Vision Transformers, investigating how attention heads specialize during sequential learning tasks.

The project explores:

- attention head importance estimation,
- selective parameter freezing,
- reducing catastrophic forgetting,
- and improving continual learning efficiency.
